Getting started

Three commands, then it is training

A node boots with the driver, the runtime and the collectives library already matched. Nothing to build, nothing to pin.

bash 
# a cluster of eight, in the region with capacity
tessera up --gpus 8 --card h100 --region eu-west-1

# your code, your data, your launcher
tessera run torchrun --nproc-per-node 8 train.py

# billing stops with the node
tessera down
